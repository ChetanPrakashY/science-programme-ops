# Research partnerships: stages

| Stage | What happens | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Identify | find groups whose published work fits the data (see `../landscape/`) | a one-paragraph fit note exists |
| First call | 30 minutes: their questions, our data, what each side wants | both sides want a second call |
| NDA | signed before any non-public data or plans are shared | NDA on file |
| Scope | a short plan: question, data needed, people, timeline, expected output | both sides agree in writing |
| Agreement | MoU or research agreement: who contributes what, data licence, who owns results, publication rights (publications still go through the review process) | signed |
| Data access | accounts or a data package delivered | partner confirms they can read the data |
| Check-ins | monthly, short, with a written note | a joint output is out (paper, dataset, talk) |
| Review | after the first output: continue, expand, or close | decision recorded |

Most partnerships stall between "first call" and "scope". The fix is to send the scope draft within a
week of the call, written by us, so the partner only has to edit.
